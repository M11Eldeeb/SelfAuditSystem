Warranty Department — Service Bulletin

Self Audit is moving
off Google Forms.

Starting with next month's cycle, the monthly branch self-audit runs through a dedicated system built for it — not a shared form link.

From Warranty Office To All branch warranty admins Applies to Every branch, every monthly cycle

Same job — audit 10 of last month's claims and show your paperwork is in order — but the form is gone. Claims are picked for you, photos attach to the exact claim they belong to, and you can see where your branch stands the moment your officer finalizes a cycle. Here's what's different and how to use it.

08

Officer review

Nothing for you to do here. AI checks each submitted answer first as a time-saver, but a warranty officer confirms or overrides every question by hand before anything is final — the AI never decides your score by itself.

09

See your result

Once your officer finalizes the branch, the cycle's score appears on your dashboard's Overview trend — same place you saw it in step 2, now including this month.

03

Rules worth knowing

Four things that decide your score

  • 10 claims, parts-only. Always drawn from the month before the audit cycle — a July audit checks June's parts claims.
  • 30-day deadline. Each cycle gives you 30 days. Whatever's still unsubmitted when it ends is automatically scored 0% — claims you already submitted keep their real answers and don't need any of this.
  • Photos stay visible until reviewed. You can reopen and view what you uploaded any time before your officer reviews the claim. After review, photos are deleted from storage to keep the system free to run — the claim record and your answers stay.
  • Branch Operations is once per cycle. It only unlocks after all 10 claims are submitted, and it's answered once for the whole branch, not per claim.

Questions about a specific claim, a locked form, or anything that looks wrong — go to your warranty officer directly, the same way you would have for the old form.
